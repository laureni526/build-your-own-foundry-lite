# 30-Day Capture Plan

*The system only compounds if it's fed. This is the minimum viable
habit — small enough to survive a busy month, consistent enough to
change everything.*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The weekly habit: one story

10 minutes, once a week, using the story card template.

-   **My writing day and time:** _________________________
    *(Be exact. "Sunday 8pm with coffee" survives; "weekly" doesn't.)*
-   **Where my stories live:** _________________________
    *(Notebook, doc, notes app — whatever you'll actually open. The
    system is the discipline, not the software.)*

## The day-30 pattern check (30 minutes)

-   **My review date:** _________________________
-   **My accountability partner:** _________________________

On that day, reread everything you've written and ask:

1.  What keeps repeating?
2.  Did new stories — from *different contexts* — back my hunch?
    -   Yes → promote it to a named pattern
    -   No → it stays a hunch on the Open Questions page. That's not
        failure; that's the system working.
3.  What new hunch appeared?
4.  Does my North Star question still fit my best stories — or does it
    need to widen?

## Then repeat

Set the next month's review date before you close the page. One story
a week is fifty stories a year — more documented evidence than most
experts capture in a career. Somewhere around month three or four,
you'll reread your stories and a principle will be sitting there
waiting for you.

**You won't write it. You'll find it.**

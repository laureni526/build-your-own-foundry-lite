# Story Card

*Copy this template every time you capture a story. One story a week
is the whole habit. Don't find a good story — find a specific one.
Specific beats impressive.*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Story name

*(A short, memorable handle — "The Tuesday the demo broke," not
"Reflections on resilience")*

## What happened

*(The concrete event. Names, stakes, specifics. Write it like you'd
tell it.)*

## What it revealed

*(The transferable lesson — what this taught you about how your work,
your people, or the world actually behaves.)*

## Where else I've seen it

*(Even a hunch. This field is how patterns eventually find you.)*

# Pattern Hunch

*When you notice the same lesson repeating across your story cards,
write it here — as a hunch, not a truth. The label matters: it gives
you permission to be wrong, and it protects your system from your own
enthusiasm.*

## The promotion rule

A hunch becomes a **pattern** only when multiple stories from
*different contexts* support it. Three stories from the same job prove
something about that job. The same lesson at work, at home, and in
your community is telling you about the world.

Until then, the default answer is: **Not yet. Keep observing.**
